# Pulse 1.8.6 — Mendeley works on any Mac with one click

- **Connect Mendeley with no setup.** On any Mac, open **Library connections → Connect Mendeley**, sign in on Mendeley's page and approve. No developer application, client ID or secret is needed.
- **How it works.** Pulse's free sign-in service (a Cloudflare Worker, `auth-service/cloudflare`) holds the one application secret Mendeley requires and only swaps sign-in codes for tokens. The tokens come back to your Mac, are stored encrypted with your other settings, and Pulse talks to Mendeley directly from then on. The service keeps no database, accounts or tokens. Each sign-in can only be completed by the copy of Pulse that started it (PKCE), and expires after ten minutes.
- **Your own application still works.** If you entered your own Mendeley application in Settings → Mendeley & Zotero, Pulse keeps using it on that Mac.
- **Verified live:** a fresh profile with no settings signed in through the service, synced a 347-record library over 4 pages, and refreshed its token through the service.

The Windows installer remains 1.8.0. The app is ad hoc signed and not Apple-notarized.
