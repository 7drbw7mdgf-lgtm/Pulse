# Pulse 1.8.3 — box selection, drag to map and concept-based bridging

## Map
- **Drag papers from the Library onto the map.** Dropping a card adds the paper to the active map tab exactly where you drop it; dragging a paper that is already on the map moves it.
- **Pan or box-select.** The floating map controls have a pan / box-select toggle. In box-select mode, drag a rectangle to select papers. In pan mode, Shift-drag also draws a box, and Shift- or ⌘-click adds or removes a single paper.
- **Selected papers become bridge seeds.** Selected papers are outlined on the map and highlighted in the Library. A bar at the top of the map shows the count, with **Find bridges** (2–8 papers) and **Clear**.
- Papers you place keep their position, including while a paper is centred, and dragging the map no longer closes the paper details.

## Bridging searches
Bridge searches now look for what the selected papers share. Pulse identifies the shared concepts first, using the configured AI, and keeps a concept only when its words appear in at least two of the selected papers; without AI it uses phrases the papers share. Those concepts run as a concept search alongside the citation and bridge searches, are boosted in ranking, and become the ranking focus unless you type your own question. The results header names the bridged papers and shows the shared concepts.

## Search bar
The **Search papers** button is gone; the search bar is wider and runs a concept search when you press **Enter**.

The Windows installer remains 1.8.0. The Apple Silicon package keeps its macOS 11+ target, PDF support and persistent offline ranker. The main backend requires Python 3.10+. The app is ad hoc signed and not Apple-notarized.
