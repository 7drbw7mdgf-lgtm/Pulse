# Pulse 1.7.0

11 October 2026

Mac installer: [Pulse-1.7.0.dmg](https://github.com/7drbw7mdgf-lgtm/Pulse/releases/download/v1.7.0/Pulse-1.7.0.dmg) (Apple Silicon, ad hoc signed).

Pulse 1.7 redesigns the paper inspector, the panel on the right. The abstract and the next step are always in reach, and the panel now holds your own reading notes and shows how a paper connects to the rest of your library.

## A header that stays put

- **Step through your library** with ‹ › at the top of the panel ("6 of 7 in Library"). The order follows the library list, including its sort and tag filters.
- **A compact identity line**: short author names (for example "Elmi A, Watson E, Sandu P +9"), year, journal and volume(issue):pages.
- **Status at a glance**: a DOI verified, unconfirmed or missing chip (with provenance in its tooltip), the discovery seed, and a reading status you can change in place.
- **One clear set of actions**: Open paper and Discover related, plus a ⋯ menu for Open full text, Look up details, Copy citation (Vancouver style), Use as discovery seed, Send to Zotero or Mendeley, and Remove from library.

## Three tabs instead of one long scroll

- **Overview** puts the abstract first, then a single line of citation metrics with their source and check date (click to refresh). Key findings follow, with a prompt to summarise them with the local AI agent when none are stored, and then key concepts (six shown, the rest on request).
- **Connections** shows how this paper links to your other papers: link type, strength and shared concepts, each opening that paper. When citations have not been mapped yet, it says so and offers Map citations. Citing papers, references and Discover related, with its options, follow.
- **Notes** is new: reading status (To read, Reading, Read), relevance to your review, your own tags and private notes, plus a shortcut to ask the AI agent about the paper.

The selected tab is remembered and the tabs support arrow-key navigation.

## Your notes stay yours

Reading status, relevance, tags and notes are saved with your library on this Mac, included in JSON exports and restored on reload. Your tags also appear in the library tag filter. Notes are not sent to search services, reference managers or cloud AI.

## Other changes

- The theme toggle sits on the same row as Import and Export.
- The inspector is slightly wider (372 px) to fit the new header.

## Checks

129 backend tests, 23 root workspace tests and 16 JavaScript suites passed (one new suite covers the inspector helpers: author abbreviation, citation text, library links, tags, status values, tab memory, list order and saving). The redesign was also checked in the browser in light and dark modes.

Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or later, and Python 3.10 or later are required. PDF extraction dependencies are included; Python, Ollama and model weights are separate installations. The app is ad hoc signed and is not notarized.
