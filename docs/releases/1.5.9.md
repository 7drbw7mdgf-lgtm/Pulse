# Pulse 1.5.9 source update

11 October 2026

Mac installer: [Pulse-1.5.9.dmg](https://github.com/7drbw7mdgf-lgtm/Pulse/releases/download/v1.5.9/Pulse-1.5.9.dmg) (Apple Silicon, ad hoc signed).

- Browse the timeline with native scrolling and visually stacked neighboring papers. Click an exposed card, scroll, or use the keyboard to select a paper and update its details.
- Scroll publication years independently. Expand a year into months and available publication dates; the rail keeps the active date in view.
- Collapse key concepts, citation metrics, abstract and literature discovery independently. Choices persist across paper selections and app restarts.
- Streamline the paper inspector and integrate its hide/show control into the panel. Remove redundant relevance and workspace-link sections.
- Place the AI agent in the top toolbar. Discovery and Reference managers have separate ribbon tabs; a second press or double-click hides their tools, and another press reopens them.
- Restore the previous navigation highlight after closing Settings or the full-screen Library.
- Remove the sidebar branding, duplicate paper preview and library sorting strip. Sort the full-screen library through its column headings.
- Remove the timeline heading block and missing-day labels, giving the paper deck the available canvas height while preserving real publication-date precision.
- Search Europe PMC and CORE alongside Semantic Scholar, retain provider/full-text information and choose up to 500 results, with 200 as the default.
- Improve discovery with focused query expansion, conservative seed identity checks, reciprocal rank fusion, BM25 and optional local semantic reranking, balanced citation evidence, strict exclusions, version grouping and result diversity.
- Add research questions, search progress/cancellation, match explanations, full-text filters and local relevance labels. Result action counts follow the selected papers.
- Add an offline synthetic ranking benchmark and refresh the README with current isolated-demo screenshots.

Validation: 110 backend tests, 15 JavaScript suites, syntax checks and isolated browser verification passed. Synthetic ranking fixtures support regression testing and do not establish real-world retrieval accuracy.

Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or later, and Python 3.10 or later are required. PDF extraction dependencies are included; Python, Ollama and model weights are separate installations. The app is ad hoc signed and is not notarized.

Mendeley RIS export is available. Direct sign-in and automatic sync still require the owner's Mendeley registration and HTTPS service deployment; the default installer contains no activated service.
