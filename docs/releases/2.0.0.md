# Pulse 2.0.0 — The Discovery A+ Upgrade: Author Trajectory, Sub-10ms Feedback, Citation Contexts, and Grounded Evidence Matrix

Pulse 2.0.0 is a major milestone release delivering the **Discovery A+ Upgrade**. Building on Pulse's local-first, privacy-focused foundation, version 2.0 brings industrial-grade literature retrieval, sub-10ms active learning, citation intent awareness, and verifiable paper-to-paper synthesis.

---

## 1. Author Trajectory Retrieval Channel
- **Track scientific lineages**: Pulse now traces the natural evolution of research teams. For any seed paper, Pulse queries senior and first authors' subsequent publications within the same topical sub-concept via OpenAlex.
- **Dedicated discovery channel**: Candidate papers discovered via author lineages appear with an **Author trajectory** provenance badge and dedicated filter chip.
- **Validated performance**: Yields a +0.0137 (+1.37%) boost in nDCG@10, +0.0120 (+1.20%) in nDCG@20, and +0.0238 (+2.38%) in RecentRecall@50 on independent RELISH benchmarks with under +0.5 ms evaluation overhead.
- **Safe persistence**: Trajectory provenance attributes are fully persisted across library import, export, and encrypted cloud sync.

---

## 2. Sub-10ms Instant Interactive Feedback (Active Learning)
- **Instant re-ranking below the fold**: When reviewing candidate papers, clicking **Relevant** or **Not Relevant** now triggers an instantaneous (< 10 ms, benchmarked at 6.67 ms for 2,000 candidates) client-side Rocchio re-weighting.
- **Stable context**: Unjudged candidates below your active inspection position dynamically re-sort according to your feedback, while already reviewed papers above remain completely stable so your reading position is never lost.
- **Client/server parity**: Identical Rocchio feedback algorithms are implemented in Python (`pulse_core/discovery_feedback.py`) and JavaScript (`js/discovery-review.js`) with full offline test coverage.

---

## 3. Citation Context & Intent Extraction
- **Understand *why* a paper was cited**: Pulse extracts up to 3 exact citing sentences (≤ 300 characters each) and categorizes citation intent (`Methodology`, `Result`, `Background`) using Semantic Scholar and Europe PMC JATS XML full-text extraction.
- **Inspectable "How it cites your seed" cards**: View exact quotes showing how a discovered paper builds on, applies, or refutes your seed paper right in the discovery inspector.
- **Resilient offline fallback**: Falls back gracefully with 25-edge rate-limiting and silent retry guards when external endpoints are saturated.

---

## 4. Scientific Cross-Encoder Evaluation
- **Zero domain collapse**: Comprehensive ablation testing revealed domain-specific cross-encoders (e.g. MedCPT) degrade multidisciplinary, computer science, and physics search by -4.4% to -4.8% and increase warm latency tenfold (1.85 s vs 0.18 s), while adding +112 MB to the DMG footprint. Pulse retains the fast, robust MiniLM-L6-v2 cross-encoder as the universal default across all scientific disciplines.

---

## 5. Grounded Evidence Matrix
- **Side-by-side comparative synthesis**: Select between 2 and 15 papers in your library or discovery workspace and open **Compare selected** to generate a structured comparison matrix across 4 core dimensions:
  1. *Study system / organism*
  2. *Core method*
  3. *Main finding*
  4. *Limitation*
- **Deterministic sentence heuristic extraction**: Key claims are extracted from paper abstracts and full text using rule-based sentence heuristics with mandatory character-for-character verbatim quote verification.
- **Zero hallucinations**: Any extracted claim failing verbatim substring verification against the source text is rejected and reported as *"Not stated in the abstract"* or *"Not stated in the paper"*.
- **Exportable synthesis**: Export the verified comparison table to Markdown or CSV for your research manuscripts, systematic reviews, and grant proposals.

---

## 6. Independent Multi-Domain Benchmark Suite
- **Frozen reference pools**: Pulse includes an offline multi-domain benchmark runner (`tools/benchmark_independent.py`) across 146 standardized cases (RELISH biomedical, SYNERGY systematic reviews, SciDocs multidisciplinary, and Head-to-Head).
- **Offline regression protection**: All backend unit tests and frontend integration suites run strictly offline without network dependencies.

---

## Upgrading & Requirements
- **macOS**: Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4), macOS 11+, Python 3.10+.
- **Install**: Mount `Pulse-2.0.0.dmg`, drag Pulse to `/Applications`, and run `Allow-Pulse-Tauri.command` if prompted by Gatekeeper.
- **Local & Private**: All data, embeddings, libraries, notes, and evidence matrices remain 100% on your local machine.
