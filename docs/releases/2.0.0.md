# Pulse 2.0.0 — Author follow-ups, instant feedback re-ranking, citation quotes and a comparison table

## 1. Same-author follow-ups (new search channel, on by default)
- For each seed paper, Pulse looks up recent work (last 3 years) by its first and last authors in the same OpenAlex topic. It costs at most 2 extra OpenAlex requests per seed. These papers carry a **Same senior author** or **Same first author** badge and have their own filter chip.
- **Measured on frozen candidate pools** (first-stage ranking, without the neural reranker; 95% bootstrap confidence intervals):
  - **RELISH** (biomedical, 100 seed papers): nDCG@20 rose from 0.291 to 0.302 (+0.012, CI +0.004 to +0.020). Recall@50 rose from 0.203 to 0.211. Recall@200 of papers from the last two years rose from 0.338 to 0.370.
  - **SYNERGY and SciDocs** (non-biomedical): no significant change in nDCG@20 or Recall@50. The rank of the first relevant paper was slightly lower on SYNERGY (MRR −0.009).
  - **Five-paper head-to-head** (secondary, partly circular): nDCG@20 fell from 0.441 to 0.409.
  - It meets the adoption rule in `docs/search/INDEPENDENT-BENCHMARK.md`.

## 2. Instant re-ranking when you mark papers
- Marking a result **Relevant** or **Not relevant** immediately re-orders the unreviewed results below it, using the evidence each result already carries: shared references, co-citations, concepts, authors and venue. Rows above and the selected row stay where they are, so your reading position is kept.
- **Speed:** with 2,000 results on an Apple Silicon Mac, a re-rank takes about 5–8 ms (median), 11 ms at the 90th percentile and about 13 ms for the first judgement. It is slower on older or busier machines; CI runners measured 22–37 ms for the Python version. At the default 200 results it is much faster.
- **Refine with feedback** still runs the full server re-rank. The quick re-rank in the window and the server re-rank use the same scoring and produce the same order (both are tested).

## 3. “How it cites your seed”
- The discovery preview shows up to three sentences (each at most 300 characters) in which a result cites your seed paper, with intent labels (Methods, Results, Background) and an “Influential” flag when Semantic Scholar provides them.
- **Source and coverage:** the quotes come from Semantic Scholar. Without a Semantic Scholar key, contexts are requested for at most 25 citations per seed. In a sample of 50 papers citing one seed, 26% had a quote and 6% an intent label, so labels are often missing.
- **Ranking:** a small ranking bonus for methods- or results-type citations is on by default (capped at 0.045). Its exact form has not yet been measured on the independent benchmark.

## 4. Reranker evaluation (no change to the default)
- A biomedical cross-encoder (MedCPT) was tested. It was 4.4–4.8% worse outside biomedicine, about ten times slower when warm (1.85 s vs 0.18 s), and would add about 112 MB to the download. Pulse keeps the general MiniLM-L6-v2 reranker. No model choice is offered in Settings.

## 5. Compare selected papers (automatic extraction, no AI)
- Select 2–15 papers and choose **Compare selected** for a table with these columns: study system or organism, core method, main finding and limitation.
- **How cells are filled:** fixed rules copy a sentence or phrase from each abstract (or the full text if a PDF is in your library). No language model is used, so nothing is made up. A cell is kept only if its quote appears word for word in the paper and the cell text appears in that quote; otherwise it says “Not stated”.
- **Limits:** the rules can pick the wrong sentence. On 36 abstracts checked by hand, the study-system column was correct in about 16 of 18 filled cells. Limitations are almost never stated in abstracts, so that column is usually “Not stated”. Check each cell against its quote, which is shown on hover.
- **Export** to CSV (formulas neutralised so spreadsheets cannot run them) or Markdown.

## 6. Independent benchmark
- `tools/benchmark_independent.py` re-ranks frozen candidate pools from RELISH, SYNERGY, SciDocs and the head-to-head set (146 cases) with paired confidence intervals. It measures the first ranking stage, not the neural reranker or live retrieval. Method, licences and results are in `docs/search/INDEPENDENT-BENCHMARK.md`.

## Privacy
Your library, notes and PDFs stay on your Mac. Discovery sends paper identifiers, titles and search phrases to scholarly services (now also Semantic Scholar for citation quotes). Encrypted sync (1.9.0) and problem reports remain opt-in.

## Requirements
Apple Silicon, macOS 11+, Python 3.10+. Mount `Pulse-2.0.0.dmg`, drag Pulse to Applications and run `Allow-Pulse-Tauri.command` if macOS blocks the first launch. The app is ad hoc signed and not Apple-notarized. The Windows installer remains 1.8.0.
