YOUR RESEARCH, YOUR CONTROL

Local by default.
Yours to organise.

Pulse stores your paper library on your computer by default. Optional encrypted library sync is available; imported PDFs and extracted text stay on your computer. Online discovery and reference-manager connections contact external services.
